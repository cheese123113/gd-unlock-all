# Unlock All

Unlocks everything in the icon kit and the main level list:

- **Icons**: every cube, ship, ball, UFO, wave, robot, spider, swing, jetpack, death effect, trail and ship fire
- **Colors**: every primary, secondary and glow color
- **Main levels**: removes the secret coin requirement from locked main levels

Each unlock can be toggled in the mod's settings. Nothing is written to your save: turn a setting off and the lock comes back.
